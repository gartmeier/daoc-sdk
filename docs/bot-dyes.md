# Bot dyes

The bots wore undyed armour; the 15 members of the three signature guilds wore one flat colour on every piece. [bot-dyes.sql](bot-dyes.sql) gives every PrivLevel-1 character except Niamh dyed armour and a cloak, the way players dye: [bot-dyes-undo.sql](bot-dyes-undo.sql) puts each row back.

- **Where colour lives.** `inventory.Color` per item row, loaded and saved with the item (`DbInventoryItem.Color`). A dye only copies its own `Color` onto the item and clears `Emblem` (`GamePlayerInventory.DyeItem`), so the script does the same. Dye ids are the dyes' `itemtemplate.Color`, the same id for cloth dye, leather dye and enamel of one colour (34 teal ... 140 olive gray). The player appearance packet (`PacketLib1104`) sends helm, gloves, boots, torso, cloak, legs and arms colours (`Emblem` wins when set), so others see them.
- **Look.** Slots 21-23 and 25-28 (shields left alone). Each character wears two colours that go together, the way the user remembers dyeing ("a black cloak, a golden vest"), not a realm palette: one pair from a list of about 30 (black and gold, royal blue and light gold, forest green and tan, crimson and dark gold, dark brown and tan, ...), either colour on the cloak and the other on torso, legs and arms, while gloves, boots and helm take one of the two. The choice comes deterministically from the name. The three guilds `groups/signature.ts` formed on 2026-11-04 keep their signature colour, now with a partner, and their members share the pair (cloak/body): Crimson Order crimson/dark gold, Ravens of Jordheim black/royal blue, Wolves of Tir na Nog dark gray/forest green. Starter guilds count as no guild. One unguilded character in about 16 dyes only the cloak.
- **Running it.** Only while the gameserver is stopped: it caches a logged-in character's inventory and saves the old colours back. `bun docs/bot-dyes.gen.ts` regenerates both files from the live DB (read-only; `--looks` prints each character's choice). The file names inventory rows by id, so items created after it was generated stay undyed.
- **Later gear.** Items moved through the vault or gear-care keep their colour. New items stay undyed: loot, merchant buys and a fresh `gear-template.ts` run (`/item create` from `t24_*` templates with Color 0). Run the generator again (server stopped) to dye those.
