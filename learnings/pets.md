# Pets

What each class has, how a pet is summoned, ordered and read, how it fights, how it ends. Signatures are in [sdk/API.md](../sdk/API.md) "Pets". A coordinated run's rules are in [group-coordination.md](group-coordination.md#pets-sdkgrouppetsts). `bots/examples/pet.ts` is a grind with a pet ([what it leaves to you](#fighting)).

Live on 2026-11-03 and -04 over six passes, at levels 10, 20 and 24, solo, in three small groups and in Thidranki ([rounds of three realms](rvr.md#pets-in-the-rounds)): every class below but Minstrel, Mentalist and Disciple. What only the C# or the code says reads "not seen live"; the list is at the end.

- **Level:** a share of the owner's, capped by the spell (`GameSummonedPet.cs:34-52`): 88 % for Simulacrum, spirit, Underhill, servant, turret and Theurgist's, 80 % for Hunter's and commander, 75 % for Druid's and minion. Live: L8 under a L10, L17 under a L20.

- **Base classes:** by the data, not seen live: with `start_as_base_class` only a Disciple has one from level 1, the others come at promotion, level 5, and Arboreal Path is listed for Forester and Valewalker too.

## Summoning

```typescript
const spell = bot.findSummonSpell('main');         // null if none
const s = spell && await bot.summonPet(spell);     // s.pet, s.petState, s.reason
```

- **A charm** takes a mob: `bot.charmPet(mob)`. `grind` can't choose one, so charm first, then `grind({ pet: true })`: a charmed wolf made 2 kills, one order per fight. Lose that pet and the run ends there, `not_supported`, for a class with nothing to summon. A Hunter's goes on: it tries its main pet, fights in melee on the recast, and summons when it can.
- **Cost:** a negative power is a percent of the pool. A focus caster, all these classes but Druid, Hunter and Minstrel, pays 1.2 times that with no focus item, 0.8 with a good one and the spec: 1.2 - 0.4 x (focus / spell level) x (spec / spell level), each share at most 1 (`SpellHandler.cs:1204-1221`). Live with none: a L10 Cabalist 17 of 57, a L20 Bonedancer 26 of 109, a Necromancer 13 of 109; a Hunter 26 of 105 and a Druid 25 % flat. With a focus staff: 12 for 17, 8-11 for 13, about 6 for 10, and a L10's plain L8 staff 14 for 17, the 1.0 between the two. An Animist's fixed power goes up too: Grove's Hunter 12 pays 14. `getSpellInfo` shows the base. Agility of the Lynx raises a Hunter's pool, and the percent with it: 30 of 121 against 26 of 105. Its cast times shorten too: Lesser Call 3700 for 4000, Wild Spirit 2800 for 3000. Every cast also takes about 5 endurance.
- **Cast:** about 6 s for a Simulacrum, spirit, Underhill, commander or Druid's, 19.6 s for a servant, 1.9-4.9 s for a turret, 3 s for a Theurgist's, 4 s for a charm. A Hunter's is instant. `cooldownRemaining` can read 0 up to about 1 s before the server takes one: `must_wait`, "You must wait 1 second to recast this type of spell!", `retryAfterMs` 1000.
- **Power is checked at the begin and paid at the end.** Short of power: "You don't have enough power to cast that!" in about 100 ms. Nothing is paid for an interrupted cast or a refusal at the end of the cast. A resisted charm pays.
- **The refusals at the end:** "You can't do that here!" in a capital, after the whole cast. "You already have a charmed creature, release it first!" comes at the end for a Simulacrum, turret or charm, at the begin for a commander. Under `castSpell` that end is `pet_exists`, about 6 s in with no power paid (five of five). So a release inside the cast lets it through at the normal price: a main turret released at 2359 ms, up at 3239 ms; a 6 s summon, 17 paid; a charm released 2 s in, taken at 3.9 s, on the mob it began on. `summonPet` says `pet_exists` before casting.
- **The new pet** comes 64 in front of us, defensive and on follow (`ControlledMobBrain.cs:56-57`). The window opens with no icons, and the spell's icon follows 33 ms later.
- **Its id is often the last one's.** `lastPetLoss.objectId` can equal the live pet's: compare `lastPetLoss.at`.
- **`replace`** releases only once the cast can go: not on a recast, short of power at the worst cost, or with melee on us or on the pet within the room below (at d717 on a Ruby: the mob turns on us at the release), and after what the server still runs of the last cast. A charm wants its mob 60 inside the range, as the server measures at the end of the cast: readings of 994 and 999 of 1000 lost the pet, 989 and 997 went. Beyond that it walks in first, to about 700 of 1000 (the range less 70 units/s for the cast and 20: mobs roam at 70, and one could go 280 in the 4 s), and with `approach: false` it is `out_of_range`, pet kept. A charm can still lose the pet to what the client can't see: a wrong body type, a resist, a mob that roams past the margin in the cast.
- **Room to cast:** a swing starts a timer of about 3 s, and a cast still under it at its half way ends ([spells.md](spells.md#caster-tactics)). On a 6 s cast that is any swing before the half way. On a servant's 19.6 s one only a swing from about 6.7 s on: 5.1 s in didn't stop it, 9.4 s did. A mob below us sets the timer 5 % less per level: at L20 a L7 35 %, a L2 10 %. Seven Earths in a row still failed under a L3 at L10, where 35 % should pass: a small sample. After the half way no swing does: a servant's cast finished with the owner hit from 177 to 48 health. `grind` wants a mob on its way farther off than 220 × cast / 2 + 250 (`castRoom`, about 900 for a 5.9 s cast). That has a margin for a faster mob: the real edge for a 2.9 s cast at speed 185 is about 460, its first swing with the mob reading about 200: 468 went, swing at 1515 ms, 395 didn't, 1121 ms; the rule says 569. Live: a 6.1 s summon with a mob running in at 176 went from 1380, and at 460 the first swing ended it 3.2 s in. A mob our own hit pulled counts too, by its heading, as it reads no target on its way. Below that room `grind` lets a mob running at it come, then swings, rather than walking out to it. A Necromancer goes to no melee at all: its summon goes from anywhere, as with no weapon, since no tether's cut leaves it the room for 19.6 s.
- **Our own swing holds a cast back** for the weapon's interval, up to about 5 s: "You must wait 3 seconds to cast a spell!".
- **So does a cast a hit has broken,** for 2.2 s with no swing of ours (`spell_self_interrupt_duration`, `SpellHandler.cs:372`), though a hit alone holds nothing back (`AttackerTracker.cs:154-161`). So `must_wait` with a mob on us doesn't mean we swung. Live a retry inside it read `must_wait`, in three classes with no melee in the script, one of them 2.0 s after the break. `retryAfterMs` says 2200.
- **An owner off the mesh:** the server looks for ground within 32 of the spot, and with none, or no line to it, puts the pet on the owner (`SummonSpellHandler.cs` `GetPetLocation`). It never moves then, though `walk` reads `follow` and `summonPet` succeeded. Seen with a client without navmesh and terrain. A DB move alone doesn't do it with them on: a row's z 152 too high, and the client came in on the ground, the pet 64 off and following. Walk a step first; in a worktree point `DAOC_NAVMESH_DIR` at main's `server/navmesh` and `DETOUR_LIB` at its `lib/Detour.dll`, for the client and the script.

## Commanding

`killNpc` orders the pet as well (`usePet`, default true), but not a passive one: left passive by an earlier script it stays out, and the owner fights alone (a Bonedancer hitting for 5-8, dead to two L6 ants). `petKill` leaves it to the pet, `grind({ pet: true })` keeps a window pet up and fights with it, and says `not_supported` to a Theurgist ([his pets](#death-and-loss)). The rest are single orders, answered in about 50 ms. A repeat gets its line and no 0x88, so `bot.*` reads the line first.

| Call | Line after "You command the X" | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| `petAttack`, `petKill` | "to kill your target!" | 2000 in 3D from us, wherever the pet is |
| `petFollow` | "to follow you!" | |
| `petStay` | "to stay in this position!" | |
| `petComeHere` | "to come to you." | |
| `petGoTo` | "to go to your target." | 1250 on the plane |
| `setPetAggression('passive')` | "to disengage from combat!" | |
| `setPetAggression('defensive')` | "to defend you!" | |
| `setPetAggression('aggressive')` | "to attack all enemies!" | |

**Stances:**

- **Defensive** answers whoever hits its owner, in about 33 ms, with no order, from over 1000 away, and stays on it while another enemy kills the owner (`ControlledMobBrain.cs:614-638`). `usePet: false` doesn't stop that. It leaves a mezzed mob alone. A Theurgist's pet doesn't help its owner at all.
- **Aggressive** pulls what isn't grey to the owner (`ControlledMobBrain.cs:522`) within about 500 of the pet, and ignored greys as near as 77. Any walk order to one with a target turns it defensive by itself (`CheckAggressionStateOnPlayerOrder`).
- **Passive** stops a cast at once and walks back, and stops a turret's fire within a shot or so, the owner 3000 from it. **It doesn't shed a mob on it:** the mob chases and goes on hitting, with no answer. It reaches a commander's minion too. An attack order lifts it to defensive, so it holds back only a mob far from the pet: beside it each re-order lands a swing at once, whatever stance came between.
- **A mob's own aggro** takes a pet only if it isn't grey to the owner (`StandardMobBrain.cs:528-530`). A L4 cutpurse (aggro 500) ignored a passive pet beside it for 20 s under a L10, a L8 bandit took one in about 9 s, and a L7 spiderling (aggro 200) left one alone for 15 s under a L20.

**Walks:**

- Follow stops about 80 from us, a minion 115-130, and runs at 260 to catch up. Attack, come and go run at 200.
- Come here and go to arrive exactly and stay until a follow, and `walk` keeps reading the order wherever the pet is. A pet on stay still chases a kill order.
- **After a fight** a pet on stay stays where the fight ended, unless the fight came within about 300 ms of the order. A pet on come here or go to was seen both ways: most runs stayed where the fight ended, one pass walked back. `ResumeWalkState` goes back until it has stood exactly on the spot (`ControlledMobBrain.cs:593-612`). Send `petFollow` when you want it.

**The server's target:**

- We have one selection. An order reads it and each of our swings reads it (`AttackAction.cs:84`). A cast takes its target at the begin (`SpellHandler.cs:451-453`), so moving the selection in mid-cast doesn't move the spell, though one queued behind it reads the selection at its own begin (`GamePlayer.cs:6147`): not seen live. So `petAttack(C)` moves our swing to C, and with C out of reach none land ("The river spriteling is not in view!"). `restoreTarget: true` puts it back. A refused order has moved it as well.
- An order at a realm-mate NPC answers "You can't attack a member of your realm!", at our own minion "You can't attack yourself!" (`bot.attack` at one reads `refused` with that), at a guard nothing at all. A friendly never becomes a contact, so in a run the board sends no such order.
- A release takes the selection if that is a pet of ours, else the window pet (`CharacterClassBase.cs:336-341`). `releasePet` handles that. A raw `sendPetCommand('release')` naming a stranger's NPC released the window pet.
- The 2000 is exact, in truncated ints: a reading of 1999 or 2000 can be refused. `petKill` walks and tries once more. Orders reach a pet out of view at any distance, and a heal lands on one.

**Timing:**

- **Order to first hit:** 3.7-3.8 s for a caster pet, its first cast. 3.9 s for a melee pet over 600 units, 3.8-6.8 s from 1000-1500. A pet beside its owner lands at the order, in the same snapshot. A Theurgist's Air rolls its stun, 25 % every 2.5 s (`TheurgistAirPetBrain.cs:12-13`, `:32`): 67 ms after its summon once, none in 4.5 s another time. Earth runs in for about 2.4 s, Ice's first cast lands about 3 s in, and one born within reach swings in 33-37 ms. A L10 mob in a group dies in about 10 s, so a pet ordered at +6 s lands nothing; with the order at +1.6 s a commander from 1100-1400 landed in 3-5.4 s, 254 damage in 6 fights.
- **Leave an order standing:** an order at a new target stops the cast in hand (`ControlledMobBrain.cs:222`), and a repeat on the same one leaves it alone (`:216`, not seen live). Eight orders at eight bears, 2.2 s apart, landed nothing for those 16 s; the first hit came 3 s after the last order. `petKill` doesn't re-order while its order stands.
- **What it can't reach:** it stands at speed 0 in attack state, with no line and no 0x88, and the state says it fights. Seen on a flying mob, a mob well under the water and a hill. A surface swimmer is fine. `petKill` reads `out_of_reach` after about 8 s and sends the follow that frees it. Seen once, unexplained: a servant stood 60 s on a mob it could reach.

**Spells on it:** `castSpell` of a `Pet`-target spell with no target lands on the pet, whatever is selected. A friendly heal goes at its id (a Druid's). A heal on a full pet still costs: "is fully healed", 4-6 power gone. It doesn't walk for one: with the pet beyond the spell's range it is `out_of_range`, 1000 for a pack buff, 1500 for Bone Spurs and Bone Shanks. A pack buff on a commander lands on its minion too, also with the commander holding it already: success, `(already has it)`.

## Reading state

- `sdk.getPetState()` is the window: `aggression`, `walk`, `icons` (at most 8), `targetObjectId`. It holds out of view. `undefined` on `state.pet` means an older client.
- `sdk.getPet()` is its entry, for position, `healthPercent`, `speed`. Null beyond 6000 on the plane (seen 6008-6029), and after a long same-region jump for up to 5 s. The window stays in both.
- `sdk.getSummons()` holds the pets with no window. Window orders answer `no_pet` for those: `releasePet({ target })` reaches them. It empties at a region jump, while a fire-and-forget stays in the world and fires on.
- `state.lastPetLoss` stays until the next loss. `bot.getPetAttackers()` covers the window pet and the summons.
- **Aim:** the server's 0xA1 target field reads 0 with no attack state, as a caster pet between casts, 65535 while attacking but moving or out of its attack range, and a real id only for one standing in that range (`PacketLib168.cs:820-828`). So the entry's `targetObjectId` flickers: 49 nulls in 164 samples against 0 in the window's. A caster pet at range reads null with `inCombat` false for its whole fight. **Read `PetState.targetObjectId`:** the order's line sets it within 100 ms, and it holds through 0 and 65535. A turret's kill past 6000, where no death event comes, leaves it on the dead mob until the release.
- **A mob** reads no target while it runs, and names the pet only once it stands in reach. `getPetAttackers` misses it till then.
- **0 % is alive** for anything with over 100 max health (`GameLiving.cs:2621`): 4 of 52 kills, for 2.3-5.5 s, once 7.3 s on 3-damage hits, and one read 0 then 2. A low mob too: about 145 health read 0 on a DoT for 3.9 s. Only the death event counts. A pet at 0-1 % crawls at speed 43, at 2 % about 68 back to us, a follower having a sprint's speed (`MaxSpeedCalculator.cs`).
- **`player.inCombat`** starts at the pet's first blow, not the order: false through a caster's first 3.2 s or a 5 s run. It goes 8 s after the last, and the server's own flag at 10 s.
- **Health lags a heal:** the line comes at once, the entry's reading 400-600 ms later, at times a few seconds.

**Lines from a pet.** They come to the owner at any range. A spell's hit also arrives as a `melee` combat event with result `other`.

| What | Line | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Melee hit | "Your X attacks Y and hits for N (-3) damage!" | `youHit` |
| Crit | "Your X critically hits Y for an additional N damage! (10%)" | |
| Miss | "The X attacks Y and misses! (14%)", "and is evaded!", "The X fumbled!" | `youHit` |
| Spell | "The X casts a spell!", then "Your X hits Y for N damage!" | `spell`, `youHit` |
| Proc, damage shield | "Your X hit Y for N damage!" | `spell` |
| Its resisted spell | "Y resists the effect! (12.0%)", naming neither spell nor pet | `resisted` |
| Style | "You perform your Havoc perfectly!  (+16, GR: 0.722)", two spaces, as for our own, also Polar Rift and Numb. The hit reads result `styledHit` under the pet's id | `youHit` |
| On it | "The Y hits your X for N damage.", "The Y attacks your X and misses!", "Your X was attacked by Y and their spell was interrupted!" | `damaged`, `action`, `resisted` |

## Fighting

```typescript
const r = await bot.grind({ durationMs: 60_000, pet: true });       // fight: 'pet' is the default, 'both' swings too
r.message;       // ends in what became of the pet: `; our pet died with X at 90 %, and it is on us at 80 %`
r.lastPetLoss;   // `mob` as it stood at the loss, null if by the end it is dead or beyond 2500. calledOff, stillFighting: what the end left
```

- **The end of the message:** `; our pet died with X at 90 %, and it is on us at 80 %`, the percent at the loss and then now, is the usual form, as the hold runs to arrival; `, and it is coming for us` with it still on the way. A release or a tether reads `we lost our pet`. In shade `: we are at N % health` follows, unless the mob is beside us more than 3 s after the loss, by which time our health is melee's. Others: `; we lost our pet on its way to X`, `; our pet died first, with X at 84 %`, `; still fighting X at N %` (another mob on us, the nearest, not the hurt one), `; our pet at N %`, `; resting our pet (60 %, to 90 %)`, `; no pet: rested for X, no time left to cast it`.
- **`bots/examples/pet.ts`** ran on nine classes and does what it says. A name pattern on grey mobs picks nothing, with no hint and no way to pass a con. A charmer's loss ends it with the mob on us: the idle owner died within about 7 s, that mob at 2 %.
- **A short run ends early:** `grind` pulls nothing with less than an average kill left, at most half the run: `in 25s (duration)` on a 40 s run is `minEngageMs`. `killTimeout` under 3.7 s calls a caster pet off its first cast: at 1500 a Companion made 0 kills, 2 failures.
- **A pet's kill is ours:** full XP, and autoloot has no range (coins and items seen at 1500, coins at 4145), up to 6000 from the kill (`WorldMgr.cs:28`): at 8433 no XP, no money and no line. Grey pays nothing: at L20 a L13 pays 0 and a L14 about 32 k, and a mob grey to a L20 in the group pays its L10s nothing too. At L10 a L7 pays 2289 and 81 copper, a L8 4608. A mob within 60 s of a charm ending pays nothing either: "This monster has been charmed recently and is worth no experience.", which stands in for "You gain no experience" on a grey one as well.
- **`fight: 'pet'`** stands off, and swings as well while a mob on us is within 250 and we have a melee weapon, also with a new pet up once the mob has arrived: live the owner stood under one for about 20 s with no swing, as it read on the pet when the pass began. With no weapon and `{ resummon: false }` a lost pet ends the run at once: `no_pet`, the message saying what is on us. **`'both'`** is `killNpc`. Melee is no answer for a list caster: a Necromancer lost to a L6 bandit, a Bonedancer's branch hits for 1-2.
- **Strength at L10:** Friend kills a L7 in 30 s for 40 % of its health, Companion in 17-26 s for 5-10 % and a L8 in about 35 s for 35-60 %, spirit soldier a L7 in 27-33 s for 45 %. A Ruby's cost varies most: 32-47 s on a L8 bear, 100 to 60, 100 to 40, 61 to 4; about 10 s on a L6 bandit. A Druid's L7 takes a L6 in 33-38 s for 36-37 %, a L7 in 39-58 s for 53-76 %, and mostly loses to a L8. **At L20:** Hunter's Pet L13 wins a L12 in 40 s at -44 %, a L14 in 46 s at -61 %, loses to a L16; two in a row killed a L16 with the owner idle, 105 s, the second at 9 %. A commander takes about 59 s on a L17, 25 s on a L15 with Bone Dart. It hits 60-105 at its first swing, then about every 4 s unhasted: a L7 thrall has about 145 health, a L6 ant about 110. Bone Dart's first hit took 65 % off a L7 crab from 1750. A servant 40-48 s on a L16 wolf. Grove's Hunter 23.5 s on a L14 bear.
- **Adds are what kill a pet.** Its run-in collects aggro: three crabs joined on the way, dead in 18 s with the target at 56 %. Three L8 killed an Amber in 26.5 s against 50.4 s with fewer. A charmed mob beside its old camp is attacked at once. They can't be staged, though: none came in 6 runs meant to bring them.
- **A caster mob doesn't cast at a pet it can run to:** it runs in and melees, as a manes demon did in 4 fights of 4. At an owner the same demon stops at about 620 and casts every 2.7 s for 8-9, so a hand-over from far is what sets that up. A moss sheerie handed over stops 500-800 off, 5 s into its run at 188, runs on once its root lands, and casts again about 0.8 s after a resisted one.
- **A mob stays on a pet.** It stayed through 20 s of the owner's swings beside it, 16 arrows, and an owner at 30 health who swung first. But a first spell of the pet's that is resisted, or an owner walking close, can bring it to the owner. A heal from a third party draws a L6-9 off the fighter within seconds. **Damage decides, not the kind of attack** (`StandardMobBrain.cs:553-575`): a 57-damage nuke from 738 took a mob off a spirit soldier in about 0.5 s, and it stayed on the owner about 13 s more. A Right of Life as the opener, 24 %, kept a bear on its owner for 15 s against a turret's 5 % hits. A debuff with no damage pulls at full health (Fatigue Body, on a 5 s recast; Sap Strength); after Sap Strength the turret's first hit took it back.
- **It fights on after the script ends,** and its XP arrives later. The mob can then kill an idle owner.

**When the pet is gone the mob comes for the owner.** That was most of the test bots' deaths.

- **Which:** the mob the pet hit (`StandardMobBrain.cs:566-571`), even on its first run-in at the pet, never named: a bear at 94 % ran in from 1484 and swung 6.5 s after the release. One the pet was on its way to and never reached stays where it is, at 100 % on nobody: `; we lost our pet on its way to X`. An add that took no hit from it walks home while we stand off: five crabs read on us, four cleared within 0.8 s. Standing among L7 spiderlings (aggro 200), all six on the pet still read on us at our death 3 s after its, and we had lost 77 % before it went. Inside a mob's aggro range it can have us on its list with no hit from the pet (`StandardMobBrain.cs:73-90`). One on a passive pet at 100 % with no swing from it goes home at the release, target none and speed 70, but a single missed swing does it: on us at once from 800. A hit for 0 too: a L17 wolf read on us at 950 at 100 %, the L2 servant dead to its first hit.
- **When:** after a death it stands about 1.6 s first, after a release 0.7-1.4 s (3 of 3; snapshots lag up to 250 ms, so those are upper readings; earlier passes read that as at once). From 1500 it took 9-10 s to reach a caster, 8 s from 1800, 2.5-3.2 s from nearby. Below 33 % it slows, to 20 % of its speed at 0 % (`MaxSpeedCalculator.cs:147-150`): 57 units/s at 4 %.
- **How it reads:** the wire says no target on its way. Solo the model names it on us (`getAttackers`, `mobRunInMs`). In a group, or with another pet of ours beside it, it reads on nobody until its next swing, and the board keeps it a threat for its run from where it reads, at its speed: at 19-23 % one took 19 s, past the 15 s the board had then. A bomber's victim reads no target and stays out of `getAttackers` the whole way in. ## Death and loss

33 ms is a server tick. The two lines are "You lose control of the X!" and "You release control of your controlled target." The pet's own "The X dies!" comes only within 512, and never for one whose life runs out.

| What | Order | `cause` |
|---|---|---|
| The pet dies | 0xA2 type 0, next tick the 0x88 close and the lines | `died` |
| Release, quit | Close, lines, 0xA2, in one tick. A charm: no 0xA2, a 0xDA with the mob in realm none | `released` |
| The owner dies | 0xAE, next tick the close and lines, the pet's 0xA2 up to 200 ms later. `pet` is null at the blow | `owner_died` |
| Region change | 0xB7, close, lines, 0xA2 | `region_change` |
| Tether, leash, a death out of view | Close alone | `released` |
| Same-region jump | 0xA2 type 1. No loss, the pet jumps too. On 484-6275 units the 0xDA comes at once, on longer the client asks after the next 0xA1 | none |

- **At the moment of a death** the server still refuses a summon for up to about 110 ms before the close. `summonPet` tries once more.
- **After a release** late 0xA1s can still name the ex-pet: 4 each time, 2 of 2.
- **The owner's death** takes the window pet (`GamePlayer.cs:5988-5989`). A Theurgist's and a fire-and-forget turret outlive it, fight on, and their lines reach the corpse. A corpse can't release in its first 10 s: [death-and-rest.md](death-and-rest.md).
- **A minion** goes with its commander, in the same tick or 200 ms later. One out of view stays in `state.summons` until the window closes or its id goes elsewhere. A minion summoned with its commander out of view is ours, and runs to it.
- **A charm's end:** every mob on the pet runs at us. The ex-pet doesn't for a non-pulsing charm, though an aggressive one attacks a few seconds later anyway. Resist rate is printed: 14 % on a L8, 3.5 % on a L1 for a L10's Persuade Will. None in about 30 charms on L0-10 in a later pass.
- **What a charm refuses:** a Sorcerer's or Hunter's takes no mob in combat: one that swung or was hit in the last 10 s, or is mezzed or stunned, and for 10 s after that ends (`CharmSpellHandler.cs:339-340`, `GameLiving.cs:227`, `CrowdControlECSEffect.cs:43-46`). That refusal comes after the whole cast, and costs no power: `in_combat`, in the server's grammar, "You can't charm the druid seer while its is in combat!", also for a mob mezzed in the hold, whose entry reads `inCombat` false (mez at 2993, cast 3493 to 7427, power unchanged).
