**In-fight attention (N4, 16:06, 6e28883, sdk/group 2117 pass, import check ok).** The user at 16:00 said there was "a lot of flipping", and that a player in a fight is much less likely to notice an add.
- **What it does:** with human vision and a skill, a member that fights (its own target set, and in combat or swinging) sees a narrower cone and aware radius, and notices an enemy new to its view later. Each scales with the skill:
  - novice: cone 45°, aware radius 225, notice 3000 ms
  - average: cone 65°, aware radius 325, notice 2200 ms
  - sharp: cone 78°, aware radius 390, notice 1680 ms
  - Outside a fight it is human vision as before: cone 100°, aware radius 500, notice 800 ms.
- **What it doesn't change:** an enemy that swings or casts at the member, and its own target, are known at once.
- **The forget rule:** a member still noticing a contact doesn't make the board forget it (`sight.pending`, in blackboard.ts).
- **Off unless both are set:** it needs `--vision=human` and a skill. Omniscient sight is unchanged, and a test covers that.
- **Field run:** `-- --vision=human --skill=average`. Each member's start line then reads `skill: reaction 350 ms, switch 750 ms, mistakes 4 %; in a fight: cone 65°, aware 325, notice 2200 ms`.
