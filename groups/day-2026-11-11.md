### W1, field mode

**Milestone 1 met.** Field mode ran unattended from 12:37 to 16:40 (4 h 3 min), with all three realms roaming Thidranki at once, all 24 bots in game in every sample, and no manual restart. A 30-minute smoke run came first (12:02-12:37). It was stopped once with `groups/field.stop` to load fixes, and that stop took 75 s. The bots are now alive in Thidranki for W2's 8 v 8. Gateway and swarm stay up in W1's session.

**How it works.** `bun groups/field.ts` is private, like `arena.ts`, and kept out of git by `.git/info/exclude`.
- Each realm runs `groups/<g>/rvr.ts Infinity` with no opponent, so all three patrol round the meeting point.
- Deaths during a run release to the portal keep and walk back, inside the run.
- A wipe (every member seen dead within 6 s) winds the run down. Then `go-home.ts` runs, `pickLineup` writes the next line-up (bench counts from the ledger's sortie rows), and a fresh `rvr.ts` starts.
- The ledger `groups/field-ledger.md` gets a row per fight (from the gateway's run events: kills of enemy players, our deaths, engage or retreat orders; a fight closes after 30 s without them), plus rows for sortie, wipe, out again, stall and run end.
- Samples go to `groups/field-samples.jsonl` every 5 minutes.

**Wipes and returns** (long run):
- Albion: 10 wipes in 11 sorties. Midgard: 4 wipes in 5 sorties. Hibernia: 3 wipes in 3 sorties; its third sortie lasted 161 minutes.
- Most deaths came one or two at a time, released and walked back, so a group lost all five many times without a wipe by the 6 s rule.
- **Time from a wipe to the next patrol leg:** median 0.9 min, every return within 2 min except one of 4.5 min (Albion).
  - This is the whole cycle: wind-down, release, go-home, line-up, regroup.
  - Nobody rests at the keep. A released member comes back at full health and power, so the run's readiness check passes at once and the group goes out about 10 s after its sortie starts.
- **Bench use** (sorties per bot):
  - Albion: Merlin 11 (the required Sorcerer), the others 5-8.
  - Midgard: Sigrid 5, the others 2-3.
  - Hibernia: Brigid and Oisin 3 (both required), the others 1-2. With so few wipes, Hibernia's rotation hardly turned: it swaps only on a wipe.

**Stuck walks:** 31 walk failures in the long run.
- 29 were `stuck` with "max speed 0", meaning rooted, stunned or mezzed in a fight: crowd control, not navigation.
- 2 were real: Thorgrim at (41539, 37957) at 13:29 and Ulfgar at (46900, 31491) at 13:49, both Midgard.
- Stalls (the group's centre moved less than 400 in 5 minutes out of a fight): 1 in the smoke run and 2 in the long run, all fixed (below). None after 14:09, once every group ran the fixed code.

**Load** (49 samples; passive, not the load test):

| Process | CPU | Memory | Event-loop lag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swarm, 24 bots in one process | median 12.8 %, max 14.9 % | 106-136 MB | max 6 ms per minute window; never a run over 100 ms |
| Gateway | median 9.8 %, max 13 % | 92-115 MB, no steady climb | max 10 ms |
| Gameserver (docker) | median 18 %, max 23 % | 3.75 GB | - |

The lag is measured inside each process (`sdk/loop-lag.ts`). The swarm prints it in its status line, and the gateway serves it in `/status` as `process`.

**What broke and what was fixed** (each fix live from the next sortie):
1. **Viewer clock.** A field run showed "0:00 left": the runner's Infinity deadline turned to 0 through JSON and `num()`. Now it shows "no end time" in the viewer and the CLI (4216282).
2. **Patrol stood still.** Albion stood 10 minutes 1000 short of a waypoint. Its spread formation keeps the support 1100 back, two members stood 1300-1400 from the leg, and the leg timeout lets only one be short. Now a leg also counts as reached after 30 s when everyone is within 1800 of it (96cea93).
3. **Flapping ashore wait.** Midgard switched between "wait ashore" and patrolling every second for 5 minutes, twice. Two swimming enemies flickered at the edge of hunt range, and every tick without them restarted the 45 s wait. Now the wait is forgotten only after 10 s with no swimmer in sight (06a1f83).
4. **field.ts's own bugs,** fixed in the smoke run:
   - An "out again" row fired while the wiped run was still winding down.
   - The 5 MB `bun` shim processes were counted in the samples.
   - Fight rows were written for a threat seen for a moment with no blood (fixed in the file, used from field.ts's next start).
5. **Also added:** passive loop-lag sampling for the swarm and the gateway (a7e1703).

**Open, for whoever runs field mode next:**

- **The "rest at the keep" step does nothing.** Released members are full. If returns should be slower, as for a human, that is a strategy setting, not field.ts.
- **The ledger counts fights per group.** One three-way fight shows as up to three rows, one per realm.
- **W3's gameserver restart** (W3's note: tag `daoc-sdk-gameserver:core-ac64bb207` as latest and `up -d gameserver`, after main has the pin and submodule commits) belongs after W2's 8 v 8. It kicks every bot. Afterwards the swarm needs a restart: quit with `bun client/dev/swarm-check.ts <bots...> --seconds=0 --quit`, then start `bun client/swarm.ts <bots...>`.
